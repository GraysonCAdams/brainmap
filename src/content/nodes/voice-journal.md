## Problem

Journaling never sticks as a writing habit, but narrating the day out loud is effortless.

## Constraints

Fully local transcription and storage; nothing about my inner life touches a third-party API.

## Approach

Capture voice notes, transcribe locally, and have a local model tag entries with people, projects, and mood for later search.

## Edge cases considered

Speech-to-text mangling names of people and projects would poison search; a personal vocabulary list likely has to seed the transcriber.

## Tradeoffs

Local models trail cloud quality, and for a private journal that trade is the whole point.
