## Problem

Kids wanted multiplayer servers; hosting cost money kids don't have.

## Constraints

Zero budget, school nights, hardware I could scrounge.

## Approach

Built a free hosting service with a self-serve control panel and learned Linux administration the honest way: by breaking production.

## Edge cases considered

Abuse handling at teenage-operator scale; other people's uptime became my first pager.

## Tradeoffs

Homework versus uptime.

## Outcome

Over 100,000 users and 500,000 visitors across its run; retired when adulthood arrived.
