## Problem

A family site I maintained needed someone to keep it running: a WordPress install, a customized theme, content that didn't go stale. I was that someone, starting around age nine and continuing for nine years.

## Constraints

No budget, no other maintainer, and a role that outlasted whatever else was happening in my life across that stretch. Nine years of upkeep is a different kind of work than building something once and shipping it; it's mostly about still being there.

## Approach

A full WordPress installation running a customized version of the default theme, touched across nearly a decade rather than built once and left alone. The footer states the role plainly: "© 2006-2015 - Grayson Chase Adams, Webmaster." Not designer, not developer. Webmaster: the person responsible for a site staying up and current after the interesting part of building it is over.

## Outcome

That footer credit, dated 2006 through 2015, is the earliest dated byline of my own work that survives anywhere, my name attached directly to a year rather than inferred from context. It's a small site and a long, unglamorous stretch of maintaining it, and the maintaining is the actual content of the story.
